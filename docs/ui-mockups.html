Real availabilityThe AI checks the Google Calendars of practitioners who do this treatment and offers only free slots.

How the calendar link works

1. Practitioner shares their Google Calendar with the clinic’s service account.
2. Paste the calendar ID here and press Test calendar.
3. The bot now offers only free slots and writes new bookings as events.

Anything staff block in Google Calendar (lunch, leave) is never offered to customers.
